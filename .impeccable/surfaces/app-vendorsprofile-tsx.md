# Vendor Profile surface brief

- Mode: Persuade — help a customer trust a vendor, assess their work, and take the next contact action.
- Preserve: all vendor data, routing, favorites, sharing, contact tracking, enquiry, package, calendar, portfolio, clips, review, realtime, and persistence behavior.
- Visual direction: RoyalLagn's modern wedding editorial. Let real portfolio media lead; use Playfair selectively for vendor identity and section storytelling, Inter for controls and metadata, bridal pink only for action and trust signals, and softly layered warm-paper/midnight surfaces.
- Priority hierarchy: hero media → vendor identity and trust → contact actions and price/packages → portfolio/clips → about/services → reviews.
- Responsive: single-column, touch-first profile below 1100px; bounded editorial content and media on desktop. Maintain 16px phone, 24px tablet, and 32–48px desktop gutters.
- Quality bar: premium marketplace polish comparable in confidence and clarity to leading fashion/social discovery apps, while remaining recognizably RoyalLagn and retaining the existing flow.
- Required states: loading, unavailable/error recovery, missing hero fallback, long vendor names/locations/descriptions, empty portfolio/clips/reviews, light/dark, hover/focus/pressed, and reduced motion.
- Avoid: ornamental excess, invented claims, new architecture, new dependencies, altered business logic, or changes outside the public Vendor Profile path.
