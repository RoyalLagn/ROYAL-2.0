Direction: Premium RoyalLagn Evolution. Preserve the incumbent blush-pink palette, Inter/Playfair pairing, rounded editorial geometry, dark mode, and familiar navigation. Improve hierarchy, media prominence, content rhythm, component consistency, and tactile state quality. Avoid literal Myntra/Instagram imitation, generic component-library styling, excessive decoration, and functionality hidden for visual cleanliness.
